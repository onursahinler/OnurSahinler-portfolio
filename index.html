😅 A Little Secret About This Website

I know this website isn't very professional or visually appealing 😅 Actually, this is an intentional choice. The field I want to build my career in - software testing - is exactly about finding bugs on sites like this, reporting them, and helping make them better.

I've already listed the bugs, design flaws, and user experience issues on this site myself. Completely fixing them is the developers' job 😅. Good projects emerge through everyone's efforts. This site is actually just a small project I worked on in my free time. If you had even the slightest smile on your face while browsing my site, that makes me happy :)

By creating an imperfect site, I'm preparing myself for the situations I'll encounter in the real world. After all, people who can find their own mistakes have less trouble finding mistakes made by others 😄.
